---
title: 'Update Assistant'
openapi: 'PUT /api/v1/vendors/{vendorId}/organizations/{organizationId}/assistants/{assistantId}'
---

<Warning>
`PUT` replaces the whole assistant. Fields you leave out do not keep their current values:

- `recordCalls` defaults to `true`, so omitting it turns call recording back on.
- `summaryShortPrompt` and `summaryLongPrompt` reset to the default prompts.
- Call event hooks that are not in `callEventHooks` are removed. Extensions that are not in `extensions` are removed.

To change one field, read the assistant with [Get Assistant](/api-reference/assistants/get), change the field, and send the full object back. Keep the `id` of every hook and extension you want to keep.
</Warning>
